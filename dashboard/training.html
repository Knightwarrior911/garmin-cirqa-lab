          How the planner decides

          Pain or illness vetoes exercise prescription. Fatigue/soreness at least 7 defers training. Rest days stay rest days; recorded activity prevents adding a second session. Missing today's dated readiness prevents a structured suggestion. Readiness below 25 defers training; below 50, sleep below six hours, or fatigue/soreness at least 4 moderates it. These are conservative product rules—not validated personal thresholds. Cardio duration is capped to your available time and recent same-sport median; no baseline or beginner experience caps it at 20 minutes. No hard intervals, guessed heart-rate targets, or automatic progression.

          Readiness must be dated today and no more than 12 hours old; future timestamps are rejected. This freshness limit is also a product rule, not a physiological threshold.
