          About load & training guidance

          Exercise load does not measure all-day strain or muscle damage. Session-RPE is duration × your reported effort, in separate arbitrary units. This planner is a conservative training aid, not medical clearance or Garmin’s coaching algorithm. Pain or illness overrides the session. Recommendations require today’s readiness measured within 12 hours; future timestamps are rejected. Thresholds are disclosed product rules, not validated personal limits.
