# Product

## Purpose

The Garmin screen CIRQA is missing: a private, watch-first display with a configurable face, ordered glances and dedicated native metric screens. Measurements remain native and dated, not live wrist streams. Morning/evening reports organize those readings without pretending to be Garmin-generated reports. A separate training workspace preserves factual comparisons, optional owner-reported effort and conservative, explainable planning.

## Owner priorities

The owner bought screenless CIRQA and misses the Forerunner 970 / fēnix 9 Pro watch experience. Readable watch-style instruments take priority over a general analytics dashboard. The current goal is running preparation for HYROX on 25 October 2026; Ladder owns strength programming. Today must answer what run, how long, approximately how many kilometres and at what min/km or treadmill km/h, using today's recovery and this week's actual runs. No redundant phone-time clock, strength planner, invented strain quota or general sport selector.

## Daily flow

Sync the band to Garmin Connect on iPhone. Open the private cloud dashboard from its iPhone home-screen icon, or use CIRQA Dashboard on Windows. Read current measurements, check dated trends, optionally review activities. Both paths request throttled Garmin refresh and show when it last succeeded. Cloud mode requires owner authentication and durable private storage; it must not depend on the PC staying on or replace real data with a demo.

## Information hierarchy

1. Clock-free Today face, date/race countdown, four configurable metric fields and a direct running entry.
2. Pinnable/reorderable glances leading to full metric Overview / History pages.
3. Morning/evening summaries, with dates and explicit CIRQA provenance.
4. Train for running-only Today / This week / Race plan; History retains every activity, including strength recordings.

## Rules

- No invented recovery score, WHOOP-equivalent strain, inferred muscular load or motivational copy. Session-RPE is labeled as duration × reported effort, never added to Garmin load.
- No placeholders masquerading as measurements. Missing means missing; zero remains zero.
- Each metric carries its date. Older account history is not claimed to come from the CIRQA.
- Show units and data labels, preserve chart gaps, provide accessible recorded-value tables.
- Do not delete health history to simplify the display.
- Cloud freshness is not direct live wrist streaming.

## Watch detail and capability

Retain native readiness factor ratings, HRV baseline, Body Battery samples, sleep-stage intervals, daily heart-rate/stress samples and dated load-focus categories. Native skin-temperature deviation, naps, sleep recharge and restless moments are optional, never synthesized. Signed deviations and valid zero survive normalization. Keep large intraday series in private storage and load one selected day on demand.

The account capability audit returned no usable dated running-tolerance, race-prediction, endurance/hill-score or lactate-threshold record. Running economy was not exposed through the audited client. This is an availability finding, not a universal claim about CIRQA or compatible accessories. Do not fill these gaps with modeled Garmin scores. Calendar workouts remain distinct from native watch Daily Suggested Workouts.

The optional phone workout guide provides foreground timed steps for eligible planner sessions, including separate controlled/easy repeat segments. Pause/resume, next, reset and finish do not record an activity or control CIRQA. Each step waits for manual advancement; no background cues or live sensor data are promised. A blocked recommendation offers no guide.

## Training workspace

`/training` separates native activity load, aerobic/anaerobic Training Effect and native acute load from optional session-RPE. Missing load is unknown, not a rest day; partial totals disclose activity coverage. Compare adjacent completed seven- or 28-day periods, excluding today. Dated metric means expose sample counts; changes require at least 75% of days in both windows, a display rule rather than a significance test. Insights describe observations without assigning causes.

Optional effort (0–10), post-session soreness and notes belong to an activity and survive Garmin refresh. Running days, time budget, recent experience, surface, race date and nullable easy/tempo/weekly-distance benchmarks are explicit inputs. The date starts at the owner's stated 25 October 2026 but nothing saves automatically. Daily fatigue/soreness, pain and illness expire with the day. A single "I feel good" action explicitly means all four symptoms are absent; silence is never a symptom answer.

Policy 3 is explainable running guidance, not a clinical assessment, validated recovery model or Garmin Daily Suggested Workouts. Pain/illness, high fatigue/soreness, non-running days, a meaningful run already recorded today, stale readiness or scheduled native work can block a run. Walking alone must not. Readiness must be from today, no more than 12 hours old and not future-dated.

Today's run combines recovery inputs with the Monday–Sunday recorded week: distance coverage, runs, identifiable quality work and remaining known volume. A prior quality run this week, yesterday's run, recent strength/HIIT, missing sleep, unbalanced HRV, elevated recovery/stress or incomplete distance can remove quality/long work. Low readiness, poor sleep, low Body Battery or moderate symptoms shorten the session to recovery effort. Ladder is never reprogrammed by this app.

Quality requires regular experience and either six recent 20-minute runs spanning 14 days or an owner-supplied usual baseline of at least 8 km/week. A separated selected day can carry at most one controlled tempo/interval run, with easy running around it. Future dates are provisional and do not confer fitness. Weekly guides maintain established volume, use 90% for the specific phase and 60% for taper; race-week sessions shorten and stay easy, with no training run the day before or on race day. No automatic catch-up sessions or volume progression.

Easy pace comes from an explicit benchmark or multiple explicitly easy-rated/native-base recordings. Tempo pace is never inferred from an ordinary average. Without benchmarks, duration and effort remain usable but numeric pace/speed/distance stay unknown. With benchmarks, every step shows min/km and km/h; estimated distance sums warm-up, work and cool-down, and cannot exceed the known remaining weekly guide. Slower warm-up/recovery paces are labeled coaching estimates, not new Garmin measurements.

All running volume is kilometres. Positive-duration treadmill records with zero distance have missing mileage; the recorded week exposes coverage and cannot calculate remaining volume from partial records. Supplied usual weekly kilometres only set a planning baseline, never an actual activity total. Three complete recorded weeks can supply a volume baseline; unavailable history remains unavailable.

Legacy running setups migrate once without guessing new benchmarks. Obsolete non-running planner setups require explicit running setup; native activities, wellness readings and owner feedback remain preserved. No compatibility strength/equipment/routine controls or alternate mixed planner remain.

Garmin calendar workouts and plan availability are checked once daily across the current and next calendar month. They are not verified watch Daily Suggested Workouts. Failed checks preserve earlier schedules with an explicit error and timestamp; an empty calendar is not evidence that the watch has no suggestion.
